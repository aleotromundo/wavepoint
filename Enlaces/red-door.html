PARTNER INFORMATION

Hotel Laguna del Cocodrilo sits in front of Tamarindo Beach, surrounded by a lush tropical garden. It offers direct access to the beach and Tamarindo estuary, just a five-minute walk from downtown, where shops, restaurants and bars are located.

Hotel amenities include Red Door restaurant, known for its seafood dishes, culinary style and quality.

The Tamarindo Estuary is one of the area’s most iconic surf spots. Its sandbar favors barrel formation and offers fast, hollow sections when conditions are right. Medium swells create perfect walls for intermediate surfers; a solid swell makes it challenging, with critical takeoffs and barrel sections that require experience. The bottom changes throughout the year, so visitors are encouraged to respect local rules and lineup rotation.
