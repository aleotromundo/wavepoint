Capitán Suizo: friendly sand and waves for everyone.

Beachfront boutique hotel and home to one of Tamarindo’s favorite sandbars for learning to surf.

Visit Capitán Suizo ↗

PARTNER INFORMATION

Capitán Suizo is a four-star beachfront boutique hotel with an environmental focus and officially evaluated by the Costa Rican Tourism Institute. It has 31 rooms among native trees and spacious organic gardens that attract a wide variety of wildlife.

In a privileged setting at the end of the bay, directly on Tamarindo Beach, the hotel faces warm waters year-round and borders Las Baulas Marine National Park.

Capitán Suizo is a friendly sandbar, popular with beginner surfers and surf instructors. Its gentle, consistent wave provides a sense of safety and fun; when other spots are too big, it can also offer accessible sessions for intermediate and advanced surfers. Because of the coast’s position, it can hold a perfect offshore wind when other spots have unfavorable wind.
