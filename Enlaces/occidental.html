Occidental Tamarindo: oceanfront relaxation.

All-inclusive hotel in Tamarindo Bay, near Langosta’s coastline and consistent surf breaks.

Visit Occidental Tamarindo ↗

PARTNER INFORMATION

Located in Tamarindo Bay, in Guanacaste, Occidental Tamarindo offers a wide range of services, dining and entertainment options. It sits in front of one of Guanacaste’s most beautiful beaches and is suited to surf and nature lovers, family holidays and honeymoons.

The all-inclusive plan includes three buffet-style meals and a wide variety of snacks throughout the day. For more information, contact the call center at (+506 4600-9297) or visit Occidental Tamarindo and Barcelo.com.

Playa Langosta is a great option for those looking for good waves. It is about one mile south of downtown Tamarindo and has consistent breaks along the coast. The river mouth creates interesting waves year-round; the main peak can get crowded, while waves are often fast and variable at low tide and fuller and heavier at high tide.
