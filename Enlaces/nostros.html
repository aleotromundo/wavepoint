ABOUT THE PROJECT

Who are we?

We are two young people passionate about surfing who have found a home in the waves of Tamarindo. WavePoint grew from a shared need among local and visiting surfers: to check wave conditions before heading to a spot. We know how much the ocean can change here, so we created a free platform covering Tamarindo’s most important surf spots.

A CLEARER VIEW

Read the ocean. Choose better.

Our project covers key surf spots with high-quality cameras. Each camera is strategically placed to provide a live view of different surf breaks, helping you plan your session with more confidence, wherever you decide to go.

WHY WE DO IT

The ultimate tool for enjoying every wave.

Tamarindo is known for its variety of waves, with something for every level. Our goal is for WavePoint to help people enjoy each one. This project is our way of thanking this place for the incredible waves it has given us and making it easier to find the right one.
